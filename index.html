Studio approach

Small studio.
Large ambition.

We combine focused human creative direction with modern AI-assisted workflows to push what an independent studio can design, build, test, and refine.

01

Atmosphere first

Lighting, sound, weather, environmental motion, framing, and interaction work together so every location feels authored rather than simply filled.

02

Systems that connect

We favor mechanics that reinforce one another—exploration, progression, world state, rewards, crafting, movement, and narrative all feeding a larger player experience.

03

Iterate with evidence

Inspect, implement, playtest, compare, correct, and verify. Passing work is preserved, regressions are treated seriously, and polish is part of the process—not an afterthought.

AI-native development,
human-led direction.

Kibabu Studios uses Claude and connected AI tools as a disciplined production layer across programming, codebase reasoning, debugging, QA, regression testing, production planning, and game-system validation. Human direction stays in control: AI helps us inspect more deeply, iterate faster, and spend more time making the creative calls that define a game.
