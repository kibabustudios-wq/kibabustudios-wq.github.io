✳ 03 / The studio

Small studio.
Big worlds.

K✳

Kibabu Studios is an independent, bootstrapped game studio based in Massachusetts.

We make original IP with a hands-on, experimental spirit. Our work moves between worlds and genres, but the throughline is the same: games with a point of view, built to reward curiosity.

Creative direction stays human. We use modern tools to work more thoughtfully, test more often, and spend more time on the details that make a world feel alive.
